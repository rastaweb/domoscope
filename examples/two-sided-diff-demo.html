How This Two-Sided Approach Works

  • Left Side: Uses original content as base, highlights only removed parts in red
  • Right Side: Uses new content as base, highlights added parts in green and changed attributes with blue outline
  • Removed content is completely hidden from the right side
  • Added content and changes are not shown on the left side
  • This gives you a clean before/after view with clear visual separation
