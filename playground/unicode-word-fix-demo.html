🔧 Persian/Unicode Word Counting Fix Demo

✅ Bug Fixed!

This demo shows the fix for Persian/Arabic word counting. Previously, Persian words were not being counted in totalRemovedWords and totalAddedWords due to a regex that only matched Latin characters.

📊 Text vs Words Explanation

totalAddedTexts = Number of DOM text nodes added

totalAddedWords = Total count of words within those text nodes

Scenario Added Texts Added Words Explanation
Add "<p>Hello world</p>" 1 2 1 paragraph element, 2 words
Add "<span>Hello</span><span>world</span>" 2 2 2 separate elements, 1 word each
